Collaborate

The economics half is done.
The half that matters isn’t.

r1_Fuel has a documented impact analysis, five costed study designs, a built news corpus and a running pipeline. What it does not have is an institutional home, a qualitative lead, or anyone in a village in November. Those are the things that decide whether this becomes evidence or stays a website.

The deadline is real, and it is not negotiable BPC may run out of letter-of-credit funding in October, which makes a second hike or a duty cut near-certain. A baseline collected after that is not a baseline. W0 has to be in the field by around 20 October; P3 has to be in villages in November, before planting. Everything else can slip. These two cannot.
Open roles

Five things that are genuinely open

Not “get in touch if interested” — these are specific, and each one unblocks something specific.

Most urgent

Institutional host / PI

You’d own: Affiliation, IRB cover, and eligibility for the funders that matter.

Almost every serious funder requires either a PhD-holding PI or an institutional host. That — not money — is the binding constraint right now, and it is the reason a fundable design is sitting unfielded. A host also usually brings the one thing that would compress the timeline most: an existing sampling frame that can be re-contacted instead of built from scratch.

The ask: A department or centre willing to host, plus a senior co-investigator.

Co-lead

Qualitative / sociological lead

You’d own: The interpretive core of P1, and P2 outright.

The economics half of this is done. The half that matters — what people give up first, who inside the household absorbs it, whether the grievance is framed as class, sector, party or geography — needs somebody whose actual training is in it. Social movements, moral economy, gendered labour, or urban informality all fit.

The ask: Co-investigator on P1; lead on P2 if the e-rickshaw strand is your thing.

Co-lead

Agricultural economist / agrarian sociologist

You’d own: P3, the Boro strand — and it has a hard November deadline.

This is the channel most likely to turn into a 2027 food-price and foreign-exchange problem, and almost nobody is watching it. Planting decisions get made in November at Tk 135 diesel against a frozen procurement price. If nobody is in the field by then, the decision that sets 2027 goes unmeasured and can only be reconstructed afterwards from what people remember.

The ask: Lead or co-lead P3; a route to DAE / BADC data would be worth as much.

Technical

Bangla NLP / computational social scientist

You’d own: P4 — where there is already a corpus, a pipeline and a known problem.

1,996 articles are collected and a v0 frame classifier runs, but it is a keyword dictionary and it is not good enough: against a single-coder pass it scores F1 0.36 on government-failure framing and 0.20 on apology/empathy. It needs a BanglaBERT-class model, a validated sentiment layer, and two human coders to get kappa above 0.7. The corpus is built; the hard part is open.

The ask: Model work, or simply two coders willing to work through the codebook.

Field

Survey partner in Bangladesh

You’d own: Fielding W0 within the window.

A CATI-capable survey firm or research centre that can run a 15–20 minute bilingual instrument across 1,500–2,500 households, stratified by the main earner’s sector, with quotas that do not quietly exclude women and the poorest. Speed matters more than scale here: a smaller W0 fielded in October beats a larger one fielded in December, because the larger one is no longer a baseline.

The ask: A quote and an honest answer on the earliest realistic field date.

What already exists

What you would be joining, not starting

Built and public

  • A sourced impact analysis across inflation, fares, agriculture, industry, power and macro, with every derived figure marked and its method shown
  • A full audit log — every search, every figure, its source, and every derivation behind the analysis
  • Five costed study designs with sampling, instruments, analysis plans and ethics
  • P4’s corpus: 1,996 articles across Prothom Alo, Samakal and The Daily Star, 2022 and 2026 windows, collected politely with robots.txt and crawl-delay respected
  • A working pipeline — volume timeline, frame shares with Wilson intervals, exploratory NMF topics, a stratified coding sample and a validation script
  • A mapped landscape of institutions, adjacent researchers and funder eligibility

Known to be missing or wrong

  • No institutional host, no IRB, no PhD-holding PI — the binding constraint
  • P4’s frame classifier is a keyword dictionary and fails on most frames (F1 0.36 government-failure, 0.20 apology/empathy). Only protest holds up, at 0.95
  • No human coding yet, so no kappa — the single Claude pass is a stand-in, not validation
  • No validated Bangla sentiment model
  • No Facebook or YouTube data; Meta Content Library needs an application, YouTube needs an API key
  • Protest “events” are a news-mention proxy, not ACLED event counts
  • Corpus skews to Prothom Alo and to Dhaka-centred, centrist outlets. It is not “Bangla public discourse”
  • Zero household data. That is the whole point.

The second list is longer than the first on purpose. A collaborator who finds out about these after joining has been badly treated; the point of publishing them is that you can price the work honestly before you say yes. Full detail on the data and method page.

How this works

Terms, in plain language

Authorship

Whoever does the work is on the paper. A lead on P2 or P3 leads that paper. The existing analysis is groundwork, not a claim on anyone else’s output.

Data and code

Code and derived metadata are public. Article text stays unpublished for copyright, and anything identifying stays unpublished for ethics. De-identified survey microdata is intended for public release after the first papers.

Ethics

Consent in Bangla, recorded verbally for phone waves. The e-rickshaw and charging work touches illegal activity, so names are never stored alongside it, diaries are encrypted, and charging-point locations are never published. Partisanship is never stored alongside identifiers. Respondents get paid for their time.

If you want to forward this to someone

Short works better than long. This is the template the project uses — reuse it, it is not precious.

Subject: Rapid baseline on the 21 Sept fuel hike — seeking your advice / collaboration

Dear Professor [Name],

I’m [name], an independent researcher in [city] studying how the 21 September fuel price hike, which came two days after the National Pay Scale gazette, affects households and informal workers. Because a further adjustment looks likely once BPC’s October LC funding runs out, there is a 3–4 week window to collect a pre-shock baseline.

I’ve drafted a short proposal for a 5-wave household phone panel stratified by public, formal-private and informal sector, with an embedded framing experiment on perceived fairness [attach 2-page summary + link]. Your work on [specific paper] is directly relevant, particularly [one sentence].

Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week? I’d value [feedback on the design / a possible institutional home / advice on data access / co-investigator involvement].

Best regards,
[Name, affiliation if any, phone, link]

Name one specific piece of their work. Attach two pages, not the repo. Make one concrete ask. Send on a weekday morning Dhaka time, and follow up once after five to seven days.
